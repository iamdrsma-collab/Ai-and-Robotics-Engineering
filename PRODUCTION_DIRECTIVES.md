# Institutional & Video Production Directives

These mandatory rules govern every lecture module across all 48 courses of the **Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics Engineering** program:

1. **Official Institution Name (Mandatory Slide Footer):**
   - Exact text: `TRIUNE BIBLICAL UNIVERSITY GLOBAL EXTENSION USA`
   - Must remain permanently anchored at the **footer of every single slide** (stationary even during slide transitions).
2. **Strict No-Logo Rule:**
   - **No image logo, crest, seal, shield, or emblem** of `TRIUNE BIBLICAL UNIVERSITY GLOBAL EXTENSION USA` is permitted anywhere in the slides or video. Institutional branding is strictly typographic.
3. **Strict Secular Academic Content Rule:**
   - **No faith-related or religious content** is permitted in any narration, slide text, examples, code, diagrams, or imagery. All instruction is 100% scientific, mathematical, and engineering-focused.
4. **Simple English Teaching Standard:**
   - Narration and slide explanations must use **clear, simple everyday English** accessible to first-year undergraduates worldwide. Every technical term is defined in plain words before extensive use.
5. **Cinematic & Luxurious Visual Design:**
   - **Resolution:** 1280×720 HD (16:9, 720p, 30 fps, progressive H.264).
   - **Slide Covers:** Every course cover and section cover must feature a cinematic, luxurious dark-obsidian and brushed champagne-gold architectural aesthetic paired with realistic robotics/AI photography.
   - **Slide Transitions:** Smooth **FADE** and **MORPH** transitions aligned to natural speech pauses while keeping the institutional footer stationary.
6. **Audio & Voice Continuity:**
   - **Audio Format:** 44.1 kHz **Mono** (`1 channel`) studio-normalized AAC audio with synchronized `.srt` and burned-in captions.
   - **Selected Course Lecturer Voice (`AI.101`):** `voice-00` (auditioned and selected by the user; preserved consistently across all AI.101 modules).
